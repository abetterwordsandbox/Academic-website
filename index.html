A life among words

Words, Worlds & Other Complications

An applied linguist by profession, a writer by inclination, and an editor of my own sentences by necessity. This is where I collect my academic work, workshops, teaching resources and assorted adventures with words. Some questions have answers. The interesting ones usually need another draft.

A little about me ↗

What interests me most is how people learn, how they become more creative, and how language can help them see what they couldn't see before.

I explore these questions through writing, teaching and research, following them into curriculum design, assessment and technology. For me, these aren't separate fields of work so much as different ways of understanding and supporting human learning.

My work spans applied linguistics, English language education, teacher development and writing. I am interested in how research can inform classrooms, shape learning experiences and open up new ways of thinking.

My academic journey has taken me from English literature and the humanities to English language education, bringing together my interests in language, thought and learning. I hold a PhD in English Language Education from the English and Foreign Languages University, Hyderabad, an MA in Humanities from the University of Texas at Dallas, and an MA in English Literature from Bangalore University. These disciplinary crossings continue to shape my work in teacher education, writing development, curriculum and assessment, as well as my emerging interest in the implications of AI for language and learning.
