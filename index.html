About

What interests me most is how people learn, how they become more creative, and how language can help them see what they couldn't see before. I explore these questions through writing, teaching and research, following them into curriculum design, assessment and technology. For me, these aren't separate fields of work so much as different ways of understanding and supporting human learning.

Curiosity is a way of paying attention.
